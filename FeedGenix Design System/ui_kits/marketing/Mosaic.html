Frequently

questions

How do I open a FeedGenix account?›
Sign up with your work email, verify with a 6-digit code, then upload your first CSV. The free tier covers 500 rows and 3 languages — no credit card.
What file formats do you support?›
CSV (any delimiter), TSV, XML (Google Shopping, Facebook, custom schemas), JSON, and Excel (.xlsx). Up to 10,000 rows per batch on Pro; unlimited on Enterprise.
How does the SHA-256 hash cache work?›
Every translatable field is hashed before routing. If we've seen the same source + target pair before, we serve it from cache at 0¢. Typical second-run cache hit rates land at 60–75%.
Can I protect prices, stock and barcodes from AI edits?›
Yes — that's the Golden Rule. Any field tagged as protected is never passed to the model. It's pulled through verbatim, byte-for-byte, into the output feed.
Which AI models do you route across?›
Free-tier APIs first (OpenAI free, Google Gemini free, DeepL free), then Claude Opus and Grok for long-tail languages, brand voice, and ambiguous attributes. You can pin any model per language.
Is my catalog data used to train models?›
No. We use model providers' zero-retention endpoints where available, and we never train on your data. SOC 2 Type II report available on request.
Can I use FeedGenix if I'm not based in the EU?›
Yes. We serve merchants in 38 countries. EU data stays in Frankfurt; US data in Virginia. Choose your region at workspace setup.
How do I contact support?›
Email support@feedgenix.com, or use the in-app chat. Pro plans include a 4-hour response SLA; Enterprise gets a dedicated customer success manager.
